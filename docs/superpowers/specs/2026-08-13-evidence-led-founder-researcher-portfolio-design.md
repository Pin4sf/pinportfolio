# Evidence-Led Founder-Researcher Portfolio Design

**Date:** 2026-08-13

**Status:** Approved for implementation

**Scope:** First public-facing portfolio rearchitecture, implemented locally only

**Publication boundary:** Do not push or deploy without separate explicit approval

## 1. Objective

Reposition Shivansh Fulper's portfolio from a visually distinctive but broad builder profile into a canonical evidence layer for three overlapping audiences:

1. Founder and startup accelerator reviewers evaluating founder insight, conviction, execution, and market judgment.
2. Research fellowship reviewers evaluating technical depth, research taste, intellectual honesty, and future potential.
3. Frontier AI teams and collaborators evaluating systems experience, independent thinking, and ability to connect research to deployed work.

Research is the organizing method across all three audiences. The site must not manufacture an academic identity or turn Waldo into a safety-research project. It should demonstrate how Shivansh observes problems, studies systems, builds responses, tests assumptions, changes his mind, and defines boundaries around what evidence establishes.

The desired identity is:

> Founder + AI systems researcher

The trajectory is:

> Models -> Agents -> World

The current center of gravity is persistent agent systems: memory and state, long-horizon behavior, harnesses, context, permissions, monitoring, control, recovery, and outcome evaluation. The longer horizon is multimodal agents, physical AI, and robotics.

## 2. Product principles

### 2.1 Personality through judgment

The portfolio should not describe Shivansh with generic personality adjectives. Personality should emerge through:

- the questions he chooses to investigate;
- founder-lived observations;
- the systems he has built or operated;
- the distinctions he insists on preserving;
- the sources he trusts and challenges;
- the claims he refuses to overstate;
- the falsifiers he applies to his own product;
- the relationship between his early curiosity, current venture, and long-term direction.

The voice should be curious, technically serious, concrete, morally alert, and comfortable acknowledging uncertainty. It should retain warmth and founder energy without hype.

### 2.2 Research as connective tissue

Research is not a separate academic tab detached from the founder story. It connects:

- Project EKA to questions about model capability, data, and evaluation;
- Atlan to questions about production agent behavior, context, permissions, and reliability;
- Waldo to persistent agents, user ownership, responsibility continuity, and outcome truth;
- Smart Manufacturing and physical-system projects to uncertainty, sensing, control, and physical consequences;
- Waldo Brain to public technical essays and inspectable research synthesis.

### 2.3 Evidence before authority

Every consequential public statement must fall into one of these classes:

| Status | Meaning | Presentation rule |
|---|---|---|
| Observed | Directly experienced by Shivansh or the team | State the setting and scope |
| Built | Implemented in a real system | Name the artifact and its boundary |
| Demonstrated | Verified through a bounded test or internal use | Describe the acceptance evidence |
| Derived | A reasoned interpretation of observations and sources | Label it as an interpretation |
| Hypothesis | A proposed product or research explanation | Pair it with an unknown or falsifier |
| Direction | A long-term area of interest | Do not imply current expertise |
| Historical | Accurate to an earlier product or career chapter | Date or label the chapter |

The public portfolio must not expose private Waldo Brain paths, private user data, confidential Atlan details, or internal discussions. Public writing should cite safe primary sources wherever possible.

### 2.4 One coherent identity, not three optimized personas

The site will not have separate founder, researcher, and engineer modes. The same evidence should support each audience:

- Founder reviewers see an earned problem thesis, current venture, technical execution, and falsifiable conviction.
- Research reviewers see explicit questions, source discipline, experiments, and boundaries between evidence and inference.
- Technical reviewers see architecture, implementation depth, reliability work, and real constraints.

## 3. Audience questions the site must answer

### Founder or accelerator reviewer

- What non-obvious problem has Shivansh earned the right to see?
- Why is Waldo a credible response to that problem?
- What has the team actually built?

### Research fellowship reviewer

- What technical systems has Shivansh worked on deeply?
- Which research questions emerged from that work?
- Can he distinguish observation, hypothesis, and proof?
- Does he understand evaluation, failure modes, and counterarguments?

### Technical collaborator or frontier team

- Which system boundaries does he understand?
- Does he reason about runtime behavior, not only model capability?
- Can he communicate technical work without leaking confidential details or inflating ownership?

## 5. Waldo case-study design

Waldo remains the most prominent current work but must read as a founder's evolving investigation, not a company landing page or a retrofitted academic paper.

### 5.1 Opening

**Eyebrow:**

> Waldo · The current thesis

**Statement:**

> Machine execution is scaling. Human understanding and responsibility are not.

**Supporting idea:**

> As specialist agents, models, tools, services, and eventually machines multiply, producing work becomes cheaper while comprehension, judgment, verification, authority, and accountability remain human constraints.

### 5.2 Narrative sequence

1. World change and founder posture
2. Founder-lived evidence from Atlan and studio/delivery work
3. Two curves: personal intelligence expands while machine execution fragments and multiplies
4. Human problem pool, with epistemic problems at the center
5. Primary-source signals and their explicit limitations
6. Current response hypothesis: responsibility continuity
7. Working foundations: Kennel, durable harness, and Waldo mobile
8. Durable principles and non-negotiable boundaries
9. What has been built or demonstrated versus target architecture
10. Research questions, evidence threshold, and self-falsifier
11. Consumer and physical expansion horizon, explicitly secondary
12. Team and current artifacts

### 5.3 Current response hypothesis

> Waldo is testing whether one user-owned personal relationship can preserve intent, evidence, corrections, decisions, and unresolved consequences across many sessions and executors—while reducing the amount the person has to carry.

This is connective tissue to test, not a claimed category, proven market, or permanent interface.

### 5.4 Durable moral and product compass

The case study should make a compact set of principles memorable:

1. An assertion about the world is not the world.
2. More machine execution should leave the person with less to carry.
3. Understanding may move up an abstraction layer; responsibility cannot disappear.
4. “Done” belongs to world state and relevant acceptance, not only the executor.
5. Memory serves future agency, not maximum retention.
6. Routine management compresses; consequential judgment becomes clearer.
7. Personal context never silently becomes authority.
8. The user's accumulated operational life must remain inspectable, correctable, revocable, exportable, and portable.

These principles should be followed by concrete product consequences rather than displayed as slogans alone.

### 5.5 Self-falsifier

> If Waldo increases the number of surfaces, raw artifacts, or interruptions a person must manage per accepted outcome, it becomes another coordination loop rather than the break.

Initial response metrics:

- sessions or raw artifacts opened per accepted outcome;
- interruptions per accepted outcome.

### 5.6 Required claim boundaries

- Do not present Waldo as an AI alignment research project.
- Do not claim external product-market validation, retention, revenue, or willingness to pay without evidence.
- Do not present target architecture as shipped capability.
- Keep provider activity, evidence or verification, user acceptance, and unresolved responsibility separate.
- Keep health and body data as permissioned context, not the product category or prerequisite.
- Keep Kennel as a working surface and observatory, not the permanent company definition.
- Keep physical AI as a longer expansion horizon with low current validation.
- Do not imply that memory or inferred preference grants authority.

## 6. Public essay system

### 6.1 Purpose

The essay system turns selected Waldo Brain research into public, standalone arguments. It must preserve source discipline without leaking private vault structure or treating internal notes as public authority.

### 6.2 Standard essay anatomy

Every substantial technical essay should contain:

1. The question
2. Why it matters now
3. What Shivansh directly observed
4. What primary sources establish
5. Engineering interpretation
6. Product or research implications
7. Adopt / Adapt / Reject decisions where applicable
8. Counterarguments and unknowns
9. Falsifiers or next experiments
10. Sources and revision date

Not every essay needs visible headings for all ten parts, but the editorial review must account for each one.

### 6.3 Essay one: “An agent said ‘done.’ What actually became true?”

**Thesis:** Agent completion is a claim about execution; outcome completion is a claim about world state and relevant acceptance.

**Founder evidence:** Operating more than 30 production agent instances at Atlan and building Kennel exposed the coordination gap between session activity, artifacts, evidence, and the person's remaining obligation.

**Technical content:**

- session versus outcome versus open loop;
- artifact production versus external-state verification;
- verification evidence and freshness;
- acceptance, repair, reopening, deferral, transfer, change, and release;
- long-horizon failure and re-entry;
- evaluation beyond task-success rate.

**Boundary:** Do not imply the external world becomes true because an agent or a user declares completion.

### 6.4 Essay two: “Memory is not storage”

**Thesis:** Persistent memory changes future behavior and therefore becomes part of an agent's effective policy.

**Technical content:**

- memory versus current state versus policy;
- episodic, semantic, procedural, working, and reflective memory;
- provenance and freshness;
- correction, contradiction, consolidation, and forgetting;
- user ownership and inspectability;
- memory poisoning and authority drift;
- implications for long-horizon evaluation.

**Moral question:** What must a personal agent be allowed to remember, and what must remain under the person's ability to inspect, correct, revoke, export, or forget?

### 6.5 Essay three: “The harness is part of the agent”

**Thesis:** A model's deployed behavior is co-produced by its runtime: context assembly, tools, permissions, memory, recovery, scheduling, and observability.

**Empirical basis:** The essay should synthesize the cross-system lessons from Waldo Brain's source-backed breakdown of more than 40 agent harnesses. It must distinguish recurring patterns observed across the corpus from Waldo-specific architecture choices, and it must not imply that every referenced harness was benchmarked under controlled experimental conditions.

**Technical content:**

- comparison method and corpus boundary;
- shared harness anatomy across the studied systems;
- runtime loop;
- context builder;
- model adapter;
- tool plane and typed execution;
- state and memory;
- policy and permissions;
- error taxonomy and recovery;
- verification loops;
- observability and audit;
- provider portability and trust-boundary ownership.

**Boundary:** Do not claim the harness alone creates model capability or that every architecture choice has been empirically validated by Waldo.

### 6.6 Deferred expansion: the Harness Atlas

A future session may turn the 40+ harness corpus into a dedicated public comparison surface and an ongoing breakdown series. This is outside the core portfolio implementation.

The editorial reference is Sebastian Raschka's *The Big LLM Architecture Comparison*: establish a bounded comparison question, use consistent dimensions, explain architectural differences with diagrams, summarize each system, and update the corpus as the field changes. The Harness Atlas must develop its own information architecture and visual identity rather than copying the reference.

Candidate public dimensions include:

- orchestration and runtime loop;
- context construction and compaction;
- tool registration, validation, and execution;
- state, checkpointing, and recovery;
- memory architecture and forgetting;
- permissions, sandboxing, and trust boundaries;
- model and provider routing;
- scheduling, delivery, and background operation;
- observability, evaluation, and verification;
- subagent and multi-agent coordination;
- deployment model and data ownership;
- Adopt / Adapt / Reject implications for Waldo.

The future surface may become a long-form canonical comparison page plus individual harness notes. Before publication it will require a fresh source audit, explicit corpus inclusion criteria, diagram provenance, update dates, and a clear distinction between documented behavior, code-observed behavior, inference, and unknowns.

## 8. Content architecture

All reusable site content remains code-driven and centralized in `src/data/portfolio.ts` or focused content modules imported by it.

The implementation should introduce or refine these concepts:

```ts
type EvidenceStatus =
  | "observed"
  | "built"
  | "demonstrated"
  | "derived"
  | "hypothesis"
  | "direction"
  | "historical";

interface EvidenceLink {
  label: string;
  href: string;
  kind: "artifact" | "source" | "case-study" | "writing";
}

interface EvidenceRecord {
  slug: string;
  title: string;
  phase: "models" | "agents" | "world" | "field-building";
  status: EvidenceStatus[];
  role: string;
  summary: string;
  contribution: string;
  observableResult: string;
  questions: string[];
  links: EvidenceLink[];
  featured: boolean;
}

interface ResearchArea {
  title: string;
  maturity: "practice" | "investigating" | "long-term";
  summary: string;
  questions: string[];
  evidenceSlugs: string[];
}

interface WritingEntry {
  slug: string;
  title: string;
  description: string;
  category: "research" | "field-note" | "founder-note" | "historical";
  publishedAt: string;
  revisedAt?: string;
  readingTime: number;
  featured: boolean;
  evidenceSlugs: string[];
}
```

Names may be adjusted during implementation to fit existing conventions, but the evidence semantics must remain explicit.
