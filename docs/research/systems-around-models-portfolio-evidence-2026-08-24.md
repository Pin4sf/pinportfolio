## Inference and unknowns

- **Supported wording inference:** Calling the whole site a “fieldbook” is supported by the site's tagline (“The agent systems fieldbook”), its home-page copy, and its route structure. Calling it a “course book” is understandable but less exact than “working fieldbook” or “study guide.”
- **Supported topical summary:** Harnesses, memory, authority, recovery, evidence, verification, and completion are directly named across the live hero, metadata, and guides. Saying the work shares what Shivansh has been learning about the machinery around models is a casual editorial paraphrase, not a quotation.
- **Unknown from public evidence:** The public pages do not establish readership, adoption, learning outcomes, sales, peer review, or external validation. Do not add impact language without separate evidence.
- **Time-sensitive boundary:** The site calls itself a “Public working edition.” Counts and release status should be rechecked before any future major refresh.
